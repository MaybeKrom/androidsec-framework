Methodology

This assessment was performed through static analysis of the supplied APK, complemented by optional runtime tooling (ADB, Frida) when a compatible device was available. Findings are graded by severity (Critical / High / Medium / Low / Info) and assigned a confidence level based on the detection method. The risk score is derived from severity × confidence weights, capped at 100. Resilience controls (root/frida/xposed/debug/emulator detection) are reported as informational observations of positive defences, not as vulnerabilities. OWASP MASVS mappings are provided for each finding to contextualise results against the mobile security standard.
