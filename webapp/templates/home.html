What is androsec?

One place to inspect an APK and explain what you found.

androsec reads an application you are allowed to test. Static checks work from the file alone. Dynamic checks wait until you connect a device and confirm authorization. The report maps findings to OWASP MASVS, and the files never leave this computer.
